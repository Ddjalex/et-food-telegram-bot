ET-FOOD Workflow (BeU-Style)
Customer (via bot.py) browses menu and places an order

Kitchen checks item availability

If available → system notifies the customer to deposit the payment

Admin (via admin_routes.py) reviews and approves payment from the dashboard

After approval, Kitchen is notified to start preparing food

When food is ready, Driver Bot (driver_bot.py) is notified to pick up

Driver updates delivery status (picked up, on the way, delivered)

Admin Dashboard (real_time_admin_system.py) shows real-time delivery progress

Customer gets updates at every stage (food confirmed, payment approved, out for delivery, delivered)

Superadmin monitors the entire system flow in real-time

👥 Role Responsibilities
👑 Superadmin (Dashboard)
Can create Admin users

Has full real-time visibility of all system operations

Manages the entire platform (admins, logs, kitchens)

🧑‍💼 Admin (Dashboard)
Can create multiple Kitchen users

Manages food products, food categories

Approves customer payments

Sees real-time driver and kitchen activities

👩‍🍳 Kitchen (Dashboard)
Sees only orders assigned to them

Marks items available/unavailable

Starts preparing food once payment is approved

Notifies driver when food is ready

📦 Customer (Telegram Bot)
Browses menu, places order

Waits for kitchen availability

Gets notified to pay and tracks order live

🛵 Driver (Telegram Bot)
Receives pickup task after kitchen prep

Updates delivery status

Tracked in real-time by Admin and Superadmin
